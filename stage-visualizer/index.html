Stick moves · swipe looks · Hide tucks the panel · Settings for accessibility.

Motion and light pulse with the music. There's no strobe, but take a break if you feel discomfort. Open Settings for reduced motion, pause, and other accessibility options. Keyboard: W A S D or arrows walk; Q and E look left and right; R and F look up and down; H hides the panel (turn that shortcut off in Settings). In AR, point a controller at the floor until a ring appears, then pull the trigger to place the stage. B or Y opens the wrist menu on that controller.
