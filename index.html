Bellevue College XR Lab

XR Lab Projects

A collection of immersive learning, XR, VR, AR, and interactive projects developed through the Bellevue College XR Lab.

  • Interactive 3D

    Wing Chun: Siu Nim Tau

    Study the Siu Nim Tau form movement by movement with an animated 3D model, timeline, and playback controls.
  • WebXR

    XR Math Plot

    Plot parametric 3D curves, ride along the path, and explore the graph in the browser or in VR.
  • WebXR

    WebXR Accessibility Design

    Walk a lab of VR accessibility features — contrast, captions, comfort, audio, haptics — on desktop and in headset.
  • WebXR

    Ocean Acidification Tour

    A guided 360° tour of ocean acidification — eight rooms, video, student art, and an interactive oyster model.
